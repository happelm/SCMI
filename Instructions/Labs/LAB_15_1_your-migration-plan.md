# Lab 15.1 – Your migration plan

**Estimated time:** 35 minutes

**Goal:** Draft the migration plan for your own environment in `SCMI_Migration_Plan_Template.xlsx`.

1. Assessment: devices, applications and deployment types, GPOs, identities.
2. Target state: join type per device population, authority per workload, what stays in ConfigMgr.
3. Waves: the first three waves with scope, rollback owner, go and rollback criteria.
4. Risks: top five with mitigation and owner.
5. Phases: exit criteria and target dates – including the clean sweep.
6. Prepare three minutes: target state, first wave, biggest risk.

### Checkpoint

- [ ] A plan you can present in three minutes
- [ ] Every wave has a rollback owner
- [ ] The clean sweep has a date
